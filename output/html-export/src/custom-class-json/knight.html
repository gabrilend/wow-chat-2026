## Original Concept Notes (preserved for reference)

paladin or warrior base.

1. flash of light
3. sacred shield
5. renew
9. Riposte
deflection talent (rogue prerequisite for riposte)
paladin deflection (1% to parry)
reckoning talent
sheath of light
the art of war
warrior weapon mastery talent
devastate warrior ability
two handed weapon specialization (ret)
inner fire priest ability
inner focus priest talent
divine aegis priest talent
desperate prayer holy priest talent
mental dexterity shaman talent
aspiration priest talent

scales with intellect, scales with crit, wants agility or strength + intellect mail or plate gear. one talent that scales with strength, one that scales with agility (like, increase your spellpower by x% of your strength, and another talent that's increase your attackpower by x% of your agility)

abilities are shielding and focus focused. devastate is more about learning how to pierce opponents defences with successive strikes.

crits are exploiting weaknesses or repetitions in enemy approach.

agility increases crit for melee, int increases spellpower and spell crit, strength increases spellpower...

only knows 2h weapons, including polearms and staves.

the goal is to benefit Flash of Light casts on a single main target as much as possible. the sacred shield shouldn't be modified - it just is that powerful. though... maybe it should shield less damage, if we can do it without a dbc edit.

ideally, make sacred shield the main ability that everything else hinges around.
for healing, at least. flash of light, ideally made instant with art of war,
and with crits coming from auto-attacks not instant melee attacks so much.
maybe devastate we nix.

sacred shield increases flash of light crit chance, and crit chance gained from
that source primarily but also others is the main way of healing others. the
mana cost of sacred shield should be increased too if possible, maybe 12% of
total mana, not base mana. though, base mana and total mana at level 20 are
pretty similar.

buff spells are: arcane intellect, blessing of kings, horn of winter. excellent stat bonuses.

## Revision Notes

2026-04-05: Original concept reviewed. Several abilities removed as "not knightly":
- Devastate (removed - not knightly, too warrior-like)
- Rend (removed - bleeds aren't knightly)
- Hamstring (removed - crippling isn't knightly, they deliver mortal blows)
- Lay on Hands (replaced with Desperate Prayer - more personal/urgent)
- Auras (removed - auras are a Paladin-exclusive thing)
- Seals (removed - auto-attack focused, not seal procs)

Stat priority clarified: Int > Agi > Str (crit scaling is core identity)
