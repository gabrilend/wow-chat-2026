Development Roadmap

Overview

Everland Ghostsong is a WoW 3.3.5a private server with roguelike survival mechanics. - Max level: 20 - Talent points: Every 1/3 level - World: Empty by default - monsters spawn around players (ambush system) - Companions: Playerbots as AI party members
