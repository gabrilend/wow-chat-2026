# wow-chat-1's Server Settings, and What Today's Profiles Do

*Written 2026-10-19 by Claude (session wow-chat-2026-95), at the owner's
request, from the old server folder `/home/ritz/games/azeroth-core/azerothcore/`
before it is deleted. The table at the end is generated by
`scripts/compare-wow-chat-1-settings`; rerun it rather than editing the table.*

## Why this exists

wow-chat-1 ran on an older copy of the game server (AzerothCore, checked out
at 2024-09-18). Its settings file, `etc/worldserver.conf`, holds 97 values
tuned by hand away from that server's stock values. That file is the only
copy: the wow-chat-1 Lua scripts beside it are all in this project's history
(issue 104 copied them on 2026-01-28), but the settings never were. Before
the old folder (2.4 GB, with the 5.5 GB `backup` folder beside it) is
deleted, each setting needs a decision: bring it back into a profile, keep
it here as a reference only, or drop it.

How the 97 were found: every setting in the old `worldserver.conf` whose
value differs from the old `worldserver.conf.dist` (the stock file shipped
with that same server), leaving out five that only said where things were
on the old computer (the three database logins, the data folder, and
`BirthdayTime`, the server's first start).

## The question under every theme

The project's profiles split the work: **basic** is the stock game with
bots and comforts (CLAUDE.md: "stock game + playerbots + QoL"), and
**beta** is "the original wow-chat roguelike design". wow-chat-1's settings
are that design's settings. So the natural home for most of them is
**beta**, not basic. (Alpha is called "the wow-chat-1 relic" in CLAUDE.md,
so it is the other candidate.) Each theme below says what the settings did,
what the project does in the same area today, and a suggestion, most
likely first.

## Already handled

- **Run speed** (`Rate.MoveSpeed` 0.85): today's server split it into
  player and creature speeds; C003 sets the players' to 0.85 on every
  profile. Same intent, carried.
- **Fall damage** (`Rate.Damage.Fall` 8): C004 sets 10 on every profile.
  wow-chat-1 had 8. Keep 10, or go back to 8?
- **Kill experience** (`Rate.XP.Kill` 3): C005 sets 2 on release only;
  basic and vanilla run 1.
- **Low-level regeneration boost off** (`EnableLowLevelRegenBoost` 0): C034
  turns it off on basic only.
- **Gone from today's server:** `CloseIdleConnections` (idle players are now
  handled by `SocketTimeOutTime`), and the two-faction emote, mail, `/who`,
  friends and trade settings (`AllowTwoSide.Interaction.Emote`, `.Mail`,
  `AllowTwoSide.WhoList`, `.AddFriend`, `.Trade`). Nothing to decide.
- **Now stock:** `LeaveGroupOnLogout.Enabled` 0 (players stay grouped over a
  logout) became the stock value.

## The nine themes

### 1. Death, corpses and wear (13 settings)

wow-chat-1: no durability lost on death (stock 10%), but six to ten times
the wear from fighting (each hit taken 3% chance on one item, stock 0.5%;
absorbs 5%; parries and blocks 0.5%), repairs 25 times the price, no
resurrection sickness at any level (`Death.SicknessLevel` 255), and every
corpse lasting 10 hours (`Corpse.Decay.*` 36000 seconds; stock 1 to 60
minutes), looted or not.

Together: dying cost nothing, fighting wore gear out, and repairing was
the expense. Bodies stayed on the ground as a record of where fights were.

Today: issue 406 (death durability) plans a different death system, still
open. Basic has its own rules: white gear never repaired (B033) with
doubled durability (E036). Beta's ambush lays a dead monster to rest as
scenery (`Ambush.layToRest`), which 10-hour corpses would keep in the world
for hours, around every player, with 200 bots fighting too.

Suggestion: (a) beta takes the wear-and-repair half (no death loss, high
wear, 25x repair, no sickness) as one settings patch, and the corpse time
is decided separately, after a look at how many bodies a 200-bot beta
leaves lying; (b) reference only, and 406 decides death and wear from
scratch; (c) drop.

### 2. Money (8 settings)

wow-chat-1: items sold for 2 to 10 times stock by quality (grey/white 2x,
green 3x, blue 4x, purple 5x, orange 10x), and a guild charter cost 10,000
gold (stock 10 silver), which in practice means no new guilds;
several guild masters allowed; a new guild bank started with one tab.

Today: beta's treasure (issue 402 and 404's work) circulates every item
once, sold gear included; basic removed guild banks entirely (E039).

Suggestion: (a) beta takes the sell values with the repair cost of theme 1,
since one pays for the other (more coin from loot, more spent on repairs);
the guild settings stay stock, as the guild charter price is a "no guilds"
switch that was never written down as a decision; (b) reference only;
(c) drop.

### 3. Levelling (6 settings)

wow-chat-1 (besides three times the kill experience, under "Already
handled"): ten times the exploration
experience, rested experience earned ten times as fast in the world and
five times as fast in an inn or city, three times the talent points (pets
too), and group experience shared out to 148 yards (stock 74).

Today: beta caps at level 20 with a talent point every third of a level,
done by Lua (issue 205, completed), which found `Rate.Talent = 3` was not
enough for that design. C005 gives release 2x kill experience.

Suggestion: (a) beta takes the exploration and rested rates and the 148-yard
group sharing (an empty world rewards wandering and staying near your
bots), and leaves kill experience and talents to their own issues
(205's Lua and the level cap); (b) reference only; (c) drop.

### 4. Professions (3 settings)

wow-chat-1: up to five primary professions (stock 2), and prospecting and
milling raise their skill.

Suggestion: (a) beta takes all three: with few players, each one making more
of their own gear fits a survival game; (b) basic takes them too, as a
comfort; (c) reference only.

### 5. Combat and creatures (5 settings)

wow-chat-1: no extra miss chance from level differences at all
(`Rate.MissChanceMultiplier.*` 0, stock 11 against creatures, 7 against
players), creatures calling for help from 30 yards (stock 10) and fleeing
creatures fetching help from 60 (stock 30), and 18 seconds of breath
underwater (stock 3 minutes).

Today: issue 803 (monster accuracy) attacks the same problem differently on
basic: B005 caps the level gap at 3 and B031 moves crushing blows; issue
142 (symmetric aggro radius) is another take on creature awareness.

Suggestion: (a) reference only: 803 and 142 are the project's newer answers
and these would stack on them; (b) beta takes the assistance radii and the
short breath (both make the world more dangerous, the roguelike's point);
(c) beta takes all five.

### 6. Groups, dungeons and quests (7 settings)

wow-chat-1: anyone could enter any dungeon or raid at any level and without
a raid group, raid groups at any level (stock 10), raid members could still
finish ordinary quests, the dungeon finder was off, and the "you can't
enter" message listed every missing requirement at once, with links,
instead of one at a time.

Today: basic changes the Outland dungeons' level rules (E024, issue 155f)
and keeps the rest stock.

Suggestion: (a) beta takes all seven: with level 20 as the cap and an empty
world, closed dungeons would mostly stay closed; (b) reference only;
(c) drop.

### 7. Battlegrounds and duels (15 settings)

wow-chat-1: no deserter penalty, queue status announced in chat every 15
seconds to players only (from level 1), matches never ended early for being
uneven, experience for kills, invitations that try to keep the two teams
even (stock: invite everyone queued), dead players back every 60 seconds
(stock 30), and the restoration, berserking and speed buffs back every 10
seconds (stock 20, 120 and 150). Wintergrasp entirely off (2: no
Wintergrasp processing at all). A duel's winner got 10 honour points
(stock none).

Today: issue 629 (battleground fronts) is redesigning battlegrounds around
fronts and lines.

Suggestion: (a) reference only, handed to 629 as wow-chat-1's starting
point: the 60-second respawn and 10-second buffs change how fronts hold;
(b) beta takes the queue, deserter and experience settings now, the timings
later with 629; (c) drop.

### 8. Both factions together (7 settings, plus the 5 gone)

wow-chat-1: Horde and Alliance could talk, share channels, group, join one
guild or arena team, invite to the calendar and share auction houses.

Today: every profile is stock (the factions apart).

Suggestion: (a) beta takes all seven: with few players, splitting them into
two sides halves the people anyone can play with; (b) basic too;
(c) reference only.

### 9. Server, names and the world's look (22 settings)

wow-chat-1: at most 100 players, players never logged out for being away,
the anti-cheat (Warden) off, GMs invitable and befriendable and silent in
channels, no GM survey, names (players, pets, channels, guild charters)
held to one language's letters but without the reserved and profanity
lists, the fake-message check off and chat links allowed custom colours,
invisible players seen only by their own party (stock: their raid), item
values in the world database allowed to override the client data files
(`DBC.EnforceItemAttributes` 0: it matters only for the few item fields
the client files also hold, such as an item's look and kind; worth
checking against the project's item changes before deciding), creatures and
players seen from 333 yards on the continents (stock 90, today 100),
no sparkles or quest marks on objects, no quest points on the map, and line
of sight in battlegrounds not blocked by their objects.

Notes: the 333-yard sight is the one with a cost: three times the distance
is about ten times the area each client and the server keep track of, with
200 bots in beta. The quest marks and map points off match the empty-world
design (find things by looking). Warden off was likely for a custom or
older client; cc-test already handles Warden itself (C033).

Suggestion: (a) beta takes the world's-look settings (no sparkles, marks
or map points) and the away-logout one, and the sight distance is tried at
a middle value first; the rest stays stock; (b) reference only;
(c) drop.

## Decisions for the owner

One answer per theme is enough: (a), (b) or (c), or another. And one
question across all of them: is beta the right home for wow-chat-1's
settings, or alpha ("the wow-chat-1 relic")?

Once decided, the chosen settings become settings patches (`config/patches/C*.sh`,
gated by profile), and the two old folders can be deleted (about 7.9 GB on
/mnt/mtwo). This file stays as the record of what wow-chat-1 had.
