Barrens From Above

Thirty Horde players and their buddies quest across all of The Barrens, live: each leaves a town, circles a named place for about thirty stops fighting what it meets, and walks back to sell when its eighteen bag slots are full. Scroll on the map to magnify where you point; drag to move.

The recorded run

The same simulation drawn to a GIF, 873 × 960, one frame every 10 game seconds, 70 game minutes. Made by scripts/generate-barrens-gif from the data scripts/generate-barrens-data pulls out of the map files and the world database.

A drawing of intended behaviour, not the game's combat: health, damage and cast times are round numbers. Movement: players and buddies run 7 yards a second; monsters 10% faster, so a kiter survives on slows and blinks, not by outwalking. Roaming: each stop's bearing from the place's middle turns a tenth of a circle; its distance out is a percent of the way to the place's edge, moved 1 to 20 in or out at even odds. Fighting: a character opens from 30 yards with a cast, blinks or disengages out of melee, and kites in circles near the monster's home, away from where it last met danger. Monsters: aggro 20 yards, one less per level of difference; leash 120 yards; they come back after their spawn time. Places are tinted from the zone's grey toward red, green or blue so no two touching places match.
