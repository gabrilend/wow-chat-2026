Honor and Gold Ledger

Where gems, honor and gold bars come from and where they go on the basic profile, as decided up to 26 September 2026 (later the same day: glyphs repriced, honor uncapped, gold bars worth 4 gold, professions added). Most of it is planned, not built; each part says which. The issues that hold the decisions are named beside each flow (155p, 155t, 155u, 617l).

The range is the far ends: every sale 15% up, or every sale 15% down. Over many sales the ups and downs roughly cancel. Tenths left over carry to the next sale.
