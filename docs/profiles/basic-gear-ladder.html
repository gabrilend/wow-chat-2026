Level-60 Gear Ladder

Three gear sources on one scale, for deciding where each tier should sit on the basic profile: vanilla raids, the Outland dungeons, and Outland open-world farming, as proposed for basic or as stock. Hover any row to project its range across the chart.

Hover a creature level on the right to light up every row fought at overlapping levels; hover the axis along the bottom to light up every row whose gear includes that item level. Proposed view: solid bars are the planned values, dashed outlines the stock values they replace, and • marks a changed or new row. Right-hand numbers are the creature levels you fight for that gear: trash to boss for dungeons (after their level offset); for open-world zones, the middle half of the zone's hostile creature spawns (a few much higher-level spots, like a zone's level-70 corner, are left out). Open-world bars are the middle half of what each zone's normal creatures drop.

Stat budget

Item level means different amounts of stats at each quality. Converted with the commonly cited budget curves: green ≈ (ilvl − 4) ÷ 2, blue ≈ (ilvl − 1.84) ÷ 1.6, epic ≈ (ilvl − 1.3) ÷ 1.3, then expressed as the epic item level with the same budget. Approximate.

Ratings at 60

Ratings convert by the wearer's level: 14 crit rating is 1% at 60, about 22 at 70. Vanilla gear carries ratings too (its old "+1% crit" equip effects are +14 crit rating in this client), sized for level 60. Burning Crusade gear's ratings were sized for 70, so on a level 60 they are ~1.6× stronger than this scale shows. Proposed: Burning Crusade items' rating stats × 0.63 (14 ÷ 22.1), vanilla untouched.
