## Current Behavior

**Built 2026-09-28; compiles, not yet run.** Right after a buddy is made
and before its first save (`buddies_create.cpp`), `BuddyGiveStartingKit`
(`modules/mod-buddies/src/buddies_kit.cpp`) gives it:
- **bags** first: the owner's equipped bag slots (read from the characters
  database, so an offline owner works), split by
  `buddies_kit_split.h` over the lowest-quality vendor bag of each size:
  up to four bags, the owner's total or the largest total under it, as
  even as the sizes allow, more bags on a tie (the owner's example, 32
  slots as four 8s, is a test case);
- **gear**: at levels 20 to 29, the hand-chosen kit for its race and class (the
  vanilla profile's level-20 kit, 148h; `buddies_kit_tiers.h`, generated by
  `scripts/generate-buddy-kit-tiers`; the owner, 2026-09-28: "for the level 20
  list, can you use the one we made for the starting gear in a different
  profile? Eventually we'll manually create each of the tiers."; and "They
  were just chosen for level 20 characters... Then for 30 and up we can use
  the proposed system"); its 46 items whose required level is above 20
  come down to 20 (install step E048, generated with the tiers; originals
  saved and restored by the revert); then every
  slot still empty: the white item of the highest required level at
  or below its own that it can use (the game's own check), from what
  vendors sell, replacing the stock starting item;
- **food**, and **drink** for mana users: the best a vendor sells for its
  level, 5 of each (the owner, 2026-09-27: "they need real food"; 2026-09-28:
  "we should give 5, so they have something to buy from vendors");
- **money**: level x 1 silver, none at level 1.
Chosen from the world database when the buddy is made, not from a list;
only vendor items, so a buddy never carries what a player couldn't get.
What can't be given is logged. Checked: `scripts/test-buddy-kit` (bag
split, 512 checks, a planted tie-break bug caught); the vanilla world
database holds 556 white vendor armour and weapon items (to level 75),
203 foods and drinks, bags of 6 to 24 slots. Buddies made before this
keep what they have.

## Intended Behavior

The owner's decision of 2026-09-25, quoted in 617a: "clad in all white
quality gear. They have their level in silver, except at level 1 they
have none. They have the same number of bag slots as the player they
spawn with, choosing the lowest quality bags to match that number, spread
evenly as possible. Prefer four 8 slot bags over two 10 slots and two 6
slots."

- **Gear**: white (common) items in every slot the class can fill at its
  level, of the best level at or below the buddy's, of armour types the
  class wears. Chosen by a generator from the world database, not by hand.
- **Money**: level x 1 silver; none at level 1.
- **Bags**: the owner's current bag-slot total, split over four bags as
  evenly as the existing bag sizes allow, lowest quality first.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. A generator writing the white-gear table per class, slot and level
   band (a data file the module reads, or a world table installed by an
   E-step).
2. Kit applied by the module right after creation, before the first save.
3. Test: the bag split for every total from 0 to 4 x 36 slots.
