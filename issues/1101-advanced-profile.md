## Current Behavior

No such profile. Basic (155) is levels 1-60 with 3.3.5's abilities;
expert (156, planned) is 61-80.

## Intended Behavior

- **A profile named `advanced`**, beside basic, from the same source tree
  (`source-beta`), its own installed tree and databases, as every profile.
- **TBC in character**: its classes play as in The Burning Crusade where
  that was a different class identity: the abilities and talents of
  `docs/profiles/advanced-ability-versions.md` in their TBC form (the old
  priest racial spells, the single-target paladin blessings with their
  greater versions, the old Seal of Command), chosen ability by ability.
- **Talents past basic's** ("up to level 40 talents instead of 30"): 66
  points at 60, basic's 51 and 15 more: +10 for the main tree (its two
  deeper tiers) and +5 for the second (one tier more); how they arrive by
  level is a decision below.
- **Every class on every race** (1101a).
- **Northrend, at 60-70**: Northrend's creatures scaled to levels 60-70,
  its quests and dungeons with them, so the adventure goes there instead of
  (or as well as) Outland.
- **The old racial abilities** where they had their own shape (the blood
  elves' TBC Mana Tap and its Arcane Torrent, and the like), beside the
  class abilities (the owner, 2026-10-27: "we'll need the old mana tap
  system for Advanced profile, and similars"); added to the report.
- **The 3.3.5 client** stays; what changes is the server's data.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. The profile registered (`scripts/profiles`, its config patches, its
   databases), as cc-test was (issue 176).
2. The ability report's list, each ability a `spell_dbc` row or a talent
   change, applied as SQL install steps gated on `advanced`.
3. Northrend's level scaling: the creature and quest levels of Northrend's
   zones moved to 60-70 (the way basic moved Outland's, 155f, 155j),
   generated by a tool from the stock data.
4. Talent tiers: the talent points per level and the tier limit.

## Open Questions

1. (Answered 2026-10-27, then changed) **Levels**: 1 to 60, as basic (the
   owner first said "should start characters at level 40", then: "I changed
   my mind on this, let's do 1 - 60"). Northrend's scaling (Intended) to be
   set again for this range.
2. (Answered 2026-10-27: "yes northrend, no outland, on advanced") Northrend
   scaled into 1-60; Outland closed on advanced. At which levels Northrend
   begins (answered 2026-10-27: "the same as basic's Outland"): Northrend
   takes Outland's place in basic's design, a hunting ground open at the
   top level, with its creatures at the levels basic gives Outland's (the
   open world raised as basic's Outland world levels step does it, the
   dungeons on a ladder as basic's Outland dungeon ladder does,
   `docs/profiles/basic.md` lists both steps). Read by the scaling tool
   from basic's two steps, not copied.
3. **Talent points** (the owner, 2026-10-27: "it's 51 (basic's total) + 10
   + 5, 10 for the primary because 2 new talent tiers, and 5 for the
   secondary because we want +1 tier for the secondary. maybe for those we
   do +1 talent points at levels 1-10?"). The target: **66** at 60, 15 more
   than basic's 51 (one a level from 10 to 60). One extra point at each of
   levels 1-10 gives 10 of the 15. Ways to the other 5:
   - (a) one extra at each of levels 11-15 (so levels 1-9 give one, 10-15
     give two, then one a level): 9 + 12 + 45 = 66;
   - (b) one extra every tenth level (20, 30, 40, 50, 60) after the first
     ten: 10 + 51 + 5 = 66;
   - (c) the first ten levels give 1.5 each (three every two levels):
     15 + 51 = 66.
   (Answered 2026-10-27: (b).) One extra point at each of levels 1-10, and
   one more at levels 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60.
4. **Buddies**: the bot module plays 3.3.5's abilities; old forms need their
   own strategies before buddies can use them.
