## Origin

Verbatim, 2026-10-04: "Ideally, we'd also have Zul'Aman and Zul'Gurub as
another raid tier, maybe in the early 50s?"

## Current Behavior

Not built. Zul'Gurub (map 309) is a stock level-60 twenty-player raid,
its epics item level 65-68 on the ladder; Zul'Aman (map 568) is a stock
level-70 ten-player raid with Burning Crusade loot.

## Intended Behavior

The two troll raids become one raid tier for the early fifties: their
monsters set to a level a group in its early fifties can fight (155f's
level-step method), their loot rescaled onto the ladder below the
level-60 dungeons (155k's method), entered without anything a level-50s
character can't reach.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. The level steps for both maps.
2. The loot's place on the ladder (open question 1), then the rescale.
3. Entry checks (Zul'Aman's door, any level requirements on the zones'
   portals).
