## Current Behavior

**Hands by spec: already in the outfits, not yet in the server.** The
white outfits (`src/lua-basic/data/gearsets/buddy-white-<tier>.lua`,
2026-10-05) choose the weapon arrangement by the talent shape's main
tree: a warrior two-thirds protection holds a one-hander and a shield
(Flail and Reinforced Targe at level 20), a fury warrior a pair, an arms
warrior a two-hander. The running server (built 2026-10-03) predates the
outfits and still picks a buddy's gear live by the older rules, so the
owner's buddies show none of this. The build of 2026-10-12 reads the
outfits.

**Weapon family by race: not done.** The outfits are keyed by talent
shape and level only, so every race of a class gets the same weapon. The
starter kits (148h) give each race and class a weapon culture: a tauren
or orc shaman a mace, a troll shaman an axe, a blood elf paladin a sword,
a human paladin a maul, a dwarf warrior a polearm, a night elf priest a
staff. Ranged in the starter kits: a bow for every hunter, a wand for
priests, mages and warlocks; none for warriors, rogues, paladins,
shamans, druids.

## Intended Behavior

A buddy's weapons follow two rules at once:
1. **Hands by spec** (as the outfits do now): the main tree decides a
   two-hander, a one-hander and a shield, a one-hander and a held
   off-hand, or a pair.
2. **Family by race and class** (148h's table): the weapon is of that
   race and class's family, in the hands the spec asks for. A tauren
   shaman healing holds a one-handed mace and a shield; enhancement
   holds a two-handed mace before 40 and two maces from 40. A protection
   warrior of a race whose family is a two-handed polearm, which has no
   one-handed form, takes the nearest family (open question 1).
3. **Ranged by class and race**, from the same table: bows for hunters,
   wands for priests, mages and warlocks (open question 2 for the
   others).

The outfit files gain race as a key (shape, race, level), generated by
the same translator.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. Answer the open questions.
2. 148h's table as data: each race and class's weapon family (sword,
   mace, axe, dagger, staff, polearm, fist) and its ranged kind.
3. The translator: candidates within the family for the spec's hands;
   the outfit files keyed by race too; regenerated.
4. `scripts/test-gearsets`: every protection warrior record has a
   shield; every record's main hand is of its race's family where one
   exists.
5. In game, after the next build: Birth's Sargobras pick, and a new
   buddy of each role.
