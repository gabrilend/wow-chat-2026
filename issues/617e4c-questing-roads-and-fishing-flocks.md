## Origin

Verbatim, 2026-10-15, on 617e4b's first idea of danger (any land of the
other faction):

And on the fishing pole:

> or, sometimes, while questing, the buddy-bot can go fishing, especially
> if there's a flock in the corridor...

## Current Behavior

- No map of roads exists. The roaming core says so itself ("Roads are not
  weighed yet", `roam/buddy_roam_core.info.md`).
- No map of where quest monsters live exists either.
- 617e4b treats only dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and arenas as danger;
  the open world is sheathed, and a fight draws.
- Buddies fish only on a town visit (617e4).

## Intended Behavior

1. **Layers over the map.** Each continent gets overlapping region
   layers, generated by tools, never drawn by hand:
   - a *road* layer: where the roads run;
   - a *quest monster* layer: where creatures that some quest asks for
     (kill or loot objectives) spawn;
   - the existing town layer (617e4a's town circles).
2. **Questing** is being in quest-monster land that is neither road nor
   town. A buddy whose owner is questing has its weapons drawn; on a road
   it is sheathed unless it fights; in town, bare-handed (617e4b).
3. **Fishing flocks.** While questing, a buddy may stop to fish when a
   fishing pool ("a flock") lies in the corridor it is walking, with the
   pole in hand only for that.

## Suggested Implementation Steps

1. Answer the open questions.
2. The quest monster layer: from the world database, every creature spawn
   whose entry a quest names as an objective (quest_template's required
   creature or gameobject entries, and loot that a quest item drops from),
   blurred into regions on the explore grid's cell size.
3. The road layer: a generator over a road source (see the questions),
   written as a table like the town circles (install step, regenerable).
4. A lookup the weapons pass calls: road, town, questing, or open.
5. Fishing pools near the walking corridor: the gameobject spawns of
   fishing pools (school types), within some yards of the planned path.
6. In game: walk the Barrens road and step off into quillboar land.

## Open Questions

1. Where do roads come from? The game draws roads as ground textures in
   its map files (a road texture layer per map chunk); or the paths
   wandering guards and caravans walk (creature waypoints); or flight
   path routes; or hand-placed points with a tool (118, point-line tools).
   Which one, or a mix?
2. Is "quest mob" any creature a quest objective names, or only those of
   quests the owner has, or could take at their level?
3. "Overlapping layers to quantify regions": should a region's danger be
   a number (how many layers say danger, how strongly) rather than yes or
   no, so a road through quest land is "a little" dangerous?
4. A flock: a fishing pool (a school of fish), or something else?
