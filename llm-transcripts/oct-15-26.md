## Fishing poles: what I found
Only one character in the basic database is holding a pole in its main hand:
**Mozonn**, a level-1 paladin with about an hour played. Its starting hammer is
sitting in its backpack. So the buddy isn't created holding the pole. It gets
one in its bags at creation, which is intended (every buddy fishes). Later the
town "go fishing as a pastime" routine puts the pole in its hand and **remembers
the old weapon only in server memory**. If the buddy logs out or the server
restarts mid-fishing, that memory is lost and the pole stays in hand. At the
next login it looks like the buddy spawned with it.

**Proposed fix:** whenever a buddy logs in or stops fishing, if its main hand
holds a pole, it equips the best weapon from its bags. That way nothing depends
on memory surviving a restart.

There's a second gap: the white-gear lists for levels 2–9 have **no weapon at
all for mages, warlocks and feral druids**, so those buddies keep their starting
weapon. I'd fix the tool that generates those lists so every record gets a white
weapon for its level (staves for casters).

## What you asked for, as I understand it
1. Poles stay in bags; buddies hold a level-appropriate white weapon.
2. Level change and class change also give **coins equal to the trainer cost**

3. Level change and class change **summon a trainer for the character's class**

4. Weapons get a **strong permanent enchant** (Fiery Weapon or similar).
5. Each buddy keeps **a record for every weapon it uses: the weapon's listed
