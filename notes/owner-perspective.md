# The Owner's Perspective

Notes on how the project's owner plays, so explanations land. Read before
explaining a game mechanic, a balance change, or a design choice.

## Favourite class: Retribution Paladin

The owner, verbatim, 2026-09-26:

> can you put it in the project notes that that's my favorite class, and
> analogies often hit home surer if explained for that class's
> perspective? I like to off heal, and I have plate armor so I can take
> some hits. Otherwise I'm a generalist, slow and steady but swingy and
> impactful.

So, when explaining:

- **Reach for a Retribution Paladin's view first.** "What does this change
  for a paladin with a two-handed weapon, plate armour, and a few heals in
  reserve?" makes a better example than an abstract one.
- **The shape of that play**: a generalist; slow, heavy swings that land
  hard and uneven ("swingy and impactful"); plate to absorb hits; off-heals
  (Flash of Light, Holy Light, Lay on Hands) between swings; steady rather
  than bursty.
- **What such a player values**, useful for gear, talents and economy
  examples: Strength (damage), Stamina (the plate-wearer's cushion),
  **spell power, not Intellect** (owner, 2026-09-26: "ret paladins don't
  want int, they want spellpower": it feeds the off-heals and the holy
  damage), critical strike (the big swings), a slow two-handed weapon (the
  swing is the damage), plate from level 40 (mail before).
- **Good analogies from inside the class**: seals and judgements for
  "a standing rule and the moment it is cashed in"; a slow two-hander for
  "a large, uneven payout"; Lay on Hands for "a rare, decisive reserve";
  auras for "a background effect that helps everyone near you".
