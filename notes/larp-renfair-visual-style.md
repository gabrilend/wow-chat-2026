# A LARP / Renaissance-Fair Visual Style

A concept for a visual style, written down 2026-10-22 so it isn't lost. Not
planned for building; a choice for later.

## The idea

The world's monsters, shown as people in costume: humans playing the part
of the creature they stand in for, the way players at a live-action
role-play or a Renaissance fair dress up. The creature's role stays (a wolf
fights like a wolf, a spider like a spider); its look is a person wearing
gear that says what they are playing:

- a wolf: furs, a wolf-head hood, claws on the gloves;
- a spider: dark cloth, many-legged shoulder pieces, a mask;
- a kobold: a candle on the helm, a small pick, patched leather;
- an elemental: robes in its element's colours, a staff or torch.

## What it would take

In this game a creature's look is a model. Humans in chosen gear are
possible where a creature uses a player-race model dressed in items (the
project already dresses creatures that way: the outfit list that gives
Sargobras the Black Baron's cape, B047, and the stand-ins' baked looks,
628a). A style switch would map each monster kind to an outfit.
