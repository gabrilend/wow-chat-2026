A stand-in is a bot character made to fill a seat a player's own buddies
can't: a missing tank or healer in a dungeon, or the doubled places in a
battleground. The owner, 2026-10-29, on the test army: "Yeah we should
treat them like as if we had 64 buddy-bots."

## Dealt at random

gif: 4a

The army of 64 (`.buddy army 64`) is made of stand-ins, each of the game
master's side and level. The owner, 2026-10-29: "fully random talent spec
profile distribution." So each one gets a random race and class of its
side, then one of the ten talent shapes a buddy can have, every shape
equally likely, and its role is whatever that shape is.

## What fully random makes

gif: 4b

Over every Horde race, class and shape, drawn evenly, the roles come out
(from the stand-ins' test, `scripts/test-buddy-stand-ins`):

| Role    | Draws | Share |
|---------|------:|------:|
| damage  |   259 |   84% |
| tank    |    18 |    6% |
| healer  |    33 |   11% |

So a random army of 64 holds about 4 tanks and 7 healers.

## A stand-in's life

gif: 4c

1. **Made** when a seat needs it, dressed and gemmed for its level.
2. **In play** in the dungeon or battleground, its talent plan steering
   its gear and gems like a buddy's.
3. **Kept** afterwards, out of the world, never deleted.
4. **Met again** by chance, and raised one level a match (the owner: "one
   level.").

## Met by chance

gif: 4d

The owner, 2026-10-29: "there shouldn't be a set path, it should just
be... if you happen to see them, they'll level with you. it's random."
When a player needs a stand-in, one of the kept ones that fits (the same
race, class and role, at or below the level needed) is drawn at random
and raised to the level it is needed at. Its level never goes down.

## Filling the seats

gif: 4e

In a dungeon, a player's own buddies sit first (the dungeon draw picks
them without replacement). A seat no buddy can fill, a tank or healer
missing, is filled by a stand-in.
